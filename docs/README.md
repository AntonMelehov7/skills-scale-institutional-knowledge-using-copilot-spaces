# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

This folder contains OctoAcme's project management processes, roles, and working practices. Use this page as a starting point to understand the lifecycle and find the detailed guidance.

## Process overview

OctoAcme manages work through an iterative lifecycle: **initiate and validate** an idea by confirming the business need, measurable outcomes, stakeholders, and resources; **plan** approved work into a prioritized backlog with clear ownership, acceptance criteria, milestones, and risk mitigations; **execute** in small increments, tracking progress and blockers on a project board and validating work through reviews and testing; **release** with CI and security checks, staged verification, stakeholder communication, and rollback readiness; then **retrospect** to capture learnings and track a small set of actionable improvements.

The approach emphasizes customer value, clear ownership, collaboration across product, project, development, QA, and stakeholder roles, and data-informed decisions. Regular team and stakeholder communication, risk monitoring, and quality practices are part of delivery throughout the lifecycle.
